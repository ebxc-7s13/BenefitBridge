BenefitBridge puts government help in one place, in plain language and in your language.

Answer a few questions once. The app shows the schemes your family can likely get, why, how much they're worth, the papers to gather and where to apply on the official website.

INDIA
• 39 central schemes: free ration, Ayushman Bharat, PM-KISAN, PM Awas, Ujjwala, pensions, rural jobs, scholarships and more
• 240 state and UT schemes for all 36 states and UTs, listed separately for your state
• 300+ government services with official links and helplines: Aadhaar, PAN, passport, voter ID, driving licence, certificates, land records, ration card, electricity, police, grievances and more
• Government jobs and exams (SSC, Railways, UPSC, banks, Agniveer, India Post…) checked against your age, education and category, plus state recruitment and free training
• Helplines you can call with one tap: 112, 1930, 181, 1098, 14555 and state numbers
• English, Hindi, and Bengali, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese and Urdu

UNITED STATES
• 18 federal programs (SNAP, Medicaid, WIC, EITC, SSI…) with estimates, state tax credits and each state's official benefits site, in English and Spanish

ALSO
• My plan: papers checklist, status tracking and dates
• Updates on new rules and deadlines
• Official programs in 19 more countries

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No sign-in. No ads. No tracking. Your answers stay on your phone and are never sent anywhere. Works offline.

IMPORTANT
BenefitBridge is not a government app and is not affiliated with any government. It is an independent, open-source guide. Information comes from official government websites, which are linked for every scheme and service. Results are estimates; the office that runs each scheme makes the final decision. Applying for government schemes is free: never pay an agent.
