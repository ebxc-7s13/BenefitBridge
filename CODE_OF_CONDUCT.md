# Code of conduct

BenefitBridge serves people who are often in difficult situations. We expect everyone who takes part
in this project to be kind, patient and respectful, whatever their language, background or level of
experience.

- Be welcoming and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Never share anyone's personal information.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that break these rules. Report problems by
opening a private security advisory or contacting the maintainers through GitHub.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
