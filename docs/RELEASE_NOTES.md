## BenefitBridge 1.5.0: listen, share, bigger text, and services in your language 🔊

**Android:** download `benefitbridge.apk` below. **Any phone:** `benefitbridge.html` works offline.

### Listen
Tap **Listen** on any scheme to hear it read aloud in your language: what it is, why you may
qualify and how to apply. It uses your phone's own voices, so nothing is sent anywhere.

### Share with family
**Share** a scheme, a service or your whole list of matching schemes on WhatsApp, SMS or any app,
with the official link included. Where sharing isn't possible, the text is copied for you.

### Bigger text
Choose **Normal, Large or Extra large** text in Help. Every screen grows with it.

### Services and jobs in your language
All national services, your state's services and every job and exam are now written in Bengali,
Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese and Urdu, not only
English and Hindi.

Results are estimates; the office that runs each scheme makes the final decision. Applying is
always free — never pay an agent.
