## BenefitBridge 1.0.0

Find food, health, cash, energy and child care help you may qualify for. It's private,
free, and in English and Spanish.

**Try it on a phone:** download `benefitbridge.html` below and open it. It's one file that
works offline, with no install, no account and no internet needed.
**Host it:** unzip `benefitbridge-web.zip` onto any static host to get the installable app
(add it to your home screen).

### What's inside
- A 3-minute screener that checks **18 programs**: SNAP, WIC, school meals, Medicaid (adults,
  pregnancy, kids/CHIP), marketplace tax credits, Medicare Extra Help, EITC, Child Tax Credit,
  free tax filing help, SSI, TANF, LIHEAP, Lifeline, Head Start, child care help and housing
  vouchers
- Dollar estimates for SNAP, EITC, the Child Tax Credit, SSI and Lifeline, plus a total
  yearly value
- A plain-language reason for every result
- For every program: papers to gather (with check-offs), how to apply, the official link and
  phone number, and FAQs
- **My plan**: track each application, add interview and renewal dates to your calendar, and
  print or save a PDF plan
- **Safety and privacy**: nothing leaves the phone. There's a quick-exit button, a "delete
  everything" option, and links to 211, legal aid, immigration help, and domestic violence and
  crisis lines
- Full **Spanish**, dark mode, and accessibility checked against WCAG 2.1 AA on every screen

### Rules
These use figures in effect on October 1, 2026:
- 2026 poverty guidelines
- SNAP limits and benefit amounts for Oct 2026 – Sep 2027
- Tax year 2026 credits
- 2026 SSI and Medicare Extra Help amounts
- Current Medicaid expansion status

Results are estimates; the agency makes the final decision.
