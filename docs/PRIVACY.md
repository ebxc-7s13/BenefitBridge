# BenefitBridge privacy policy

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

BenefitBridge is a free, open-source app that helps people find government schemes, services, jobs and helplines.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** BenefitBridge has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We do not run servers that receive your answers.

## What stays on your phone
The answers you type (for example your state, age, family size and income) and your plan are stored only on your device, so you don't have to type them again. You can turn this off or delete everything at any time in **Help → Your privacy**. Uninstalling the app also deletes them. The Android app turns off Android cloud backup for this data.

## Network use
- When you are online, the app downloads a public list of news updates (`updates.json`) from GitHub. The request contains nothing about you.
- When you tap a link, the official government website opens in your browser. That website's own privacy policy applies.
- When you tap a phone number, your phone's dialer opens.

## Children
The app does not collect any information from anyone, including children.
