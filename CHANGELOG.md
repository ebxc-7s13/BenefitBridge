# Changelog

All releases are on the [releases page](https://github.com/ebxc-7s13/BenefitBridge/releases).

## 1.5.0 — 22 October 2026
- Read aloud: any scheme in the reader's language, using the phone's own voices.
- Share a scheme, a service or the list of matching schemes (WhatsApp, SMS, any app).
- Text size: normal, large and extra large.
- National services, state services and all jobs in the 11 state languages.

## 1.4.0 — 21 October 2026
- Home hub: basic details once, then Schemes, Services, Jobs and Help.
- 309 Indian government services and 64 helplines with official links (national and every state/UT); U.S. federal and state services.
- 37 national exams and job schemes checked against age, education and category.
- Colourful redesign; fixed bars overlapping the screen on Android edge-to-edge and notched phones.
- Google Play listing, graphics, privacy policy and App Bundle build; MIT license and community files.

## 1.3.0 — 21 October 2026
- Each state's schemes in their own results section; 240 state and UT schemes in all 36 states/UTs.
- 11 state languages offered after choosing the state (Urdu right to left).
- Android app built for free on GitHub Actions.

## 1.2.0 — 21 October 2026
- India state schemes and every state's official portal; U.S. state EITCs, child tax credits and benefit sites.
- Home-page updates with online refresh; directory of 119 programs in 19 more countries.

## 1.1.0 — 21 October 2026
- Choose your country first; India with 39 central schemes, a criteria-wise questionnaire and Hindi.

## 1.0.0 — 20 October 2026
- United States: 18 federal programs with estimates, My plan, reminders, offline single-file app, English and Spanish, WCAG 2.1 AA.
