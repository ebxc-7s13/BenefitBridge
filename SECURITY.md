# Security policy

BenefitBridge has no servers and stores nothing outside the user's device, but we take security
seriously — especially anything that could send people to a fake website or leak answers.

Please report vulnerabilities privately through
[GitHub security advisories](https://github.com/ebxc-7s13/BenefitBridge/security/advisories/new)
rather than a public issue. We aim to reply within 7 days.

In particular, tell us right away if you find a link in the app that does not go to an official
government website.
